A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you enquire.

A few details make it easier to find the right material.

Are all sugarcane products made from the same material?+

No. The paper range uses sugarcane paper or bagasse pulp, while kitchenware and films use bio-based plastic formulations. Glass containers have a glass body with separate lid components. We’ll confirm the composition for the item you choose.

What are the sugarcane and recycled ranges?+

Explore bio-based household goods, bags and wraps, sugarcane paper essentials and recycled household products. Materials vary by product; ask us about composition, care and documentation for your application.

Not sure which product to choose? +

Select “Help me choose” in the enquiry form. Tell us how you plan to use the product, any material preferences and your approximate quantity.

What should I include in my enquiry? +

Share the product or application, quantity and any size, material or finish requirements. For paper, include GSM if known. If you have a deadline, add it to your project details.

Can I ask about samples or availability? +

Yes. Add your request to the enquiry. Samples, specifications, availability and suitability are confirmed by the team for the selected product.
