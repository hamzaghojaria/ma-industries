PAPER & PACKAGING

A closer look at paper.

Choose the right finish.

MG means machine-glazed; MF means machine-finished. Tell us whether you need paper for bags, wrapping or corrugated packaging.

Look beyond GSM.

GSM describes paper weight. For packaging, also discuss bursting factor (BF), compression performance, shade and reel width.

Start with the application.

Share your product, box or bag size, printing needs and quantity. We can discuss the grade and specifications to enquire about.

A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you enquire.

A few details make it easier to find the right material.

Not sure which paper to choose? +

Select “Help me choose” in the enquiry form. Tell us what you’re making, the finish you have in mind and your approximate quantity.

What should I include in my enquiry? +

Share the paper type or application, quantity and any size, GSM or finish requirements. If you have a deadline, add it to your project details.

Can I ask about samples or availability? +

Yes. Add your request to the enquiry. Samples, specifications, availability and suitability are confirmed by the team for the selected grade.
